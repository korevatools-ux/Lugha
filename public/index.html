Your guides

Meet the detectives

Two friendly language detectives walk with you through every lesson. Every new word is a clue, and they help you crack it, in your language and at your pace.

The Word Detective

Calm, clever and always prepared. He plans your lessons, checks your answers and explains grammar in simple words.

The Clue Finder

Curious and cheerful. She spots the words you keep forgetting, brings them back as flashcards and celebrates every streak.

About Lugha

What it does

Lugha is a free language course for people who can’t pay for one. Short daily lessons, flashcards that remember what you forget, real voices and accents, and practice in the same format as the Goethe and ÖSD exams: Lesen, Hören, Schreiben, Sprechen.

Four steps, ten minutes a day

How it works

  1. 01

    Step 1: Pick a language

    Pick your country or any of 256 languages, from Deutsch and Kiswahili to chiShona, Yorùbá, Quechua and Tok Pisin, and start at your level.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Learn ten minutes a day

    A short lesson, a few flashcards and one dictation sentence. Your streak and progress save on your phone.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Speak and write

    Plan a trip with a speaking partner, write emails and get feedback on every mistake, in simple English.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Test like the real exam

    Weekly tests and email scoring that follow the official B1 criteria, so exam day feels familiar.
